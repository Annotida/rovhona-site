About Rovhona

Rovhona Community Centre is a community-led organisation focused on supporting vulnerable children and families in Moloto — Mogodongo Block 1. We operate community feeding programmes, deliver life-skills training (gardening, sewing, basic computing, baking) and provide psycho-social screening and referrals for children in need. Our work is driven by local volunteers, trained staff and partner organisations to ensure sustainable, measurable outcomes.

Mission: Build resilient families
Vision: Thriving Moloto communities
Values: Dignity • Inclusion • Accountability

Programs & Services

Life Skills Training

Practical vocational modules — gardening, sewing, baking and basic IT — designed to increase household income and employability.

Psycho-social Support

Screening, counseling, and referral pathways for children identified as needing additional psychosocial services.

Community Feeding

Daily and event-based catering for vulnerable children and families, nutritional planning and food distribution logistics.

Education Support

Donations of educational resources, learning support sessions and tutoring for school-aged children.

Volunteer & Capacity Building

Volunteer training, community leadership workshops and mentorship for local trainers.

Referral & Partnerships

Formal referral processes for health, social services and specialized support with partner organisations.

Impact & Results

What our work achieves

We measure outcomes across nutrition, skills uptake and social wellbeing. Our monitoring focuses on attendance, income generation from training graduates and referral closure rates.

  • Consistent community feeding reaching 300+ meals weekly.
  • Skills training cohorts with a >60% improvement in household food security.
  • Screening and referral processes coordinated with local clinics and social workers.
